## Development fixtures, not third-party content

`public/audio/dev/*.wav` are sine tones synthesised locally by
`scripts/generate-fixtures.mjs`. `public/icons/*.png` are placeholder marks
generated by `scripts/generate-icons.mjs`. Neither is derived from any
third-party work, and neither contains audiobook content.

Catalogue entries in `src/data/devCatalogue.ts` are fictional. Names of people,
titles, sources and organisations in that file do not refer to real individuals
or real catalogue records, and the `example.invalid` URLs are reserved by
RFC 2606 and never resolve.
